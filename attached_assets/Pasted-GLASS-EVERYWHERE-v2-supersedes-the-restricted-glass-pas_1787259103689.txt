GLASS EVERYWHERE — v2, supersedes the restricted glass pass. Every
UI surface becomes glassmorphic EXCEPT the motion backgrounds
themselves (films, posters, ambient backdrop), which stay
untouched. No motion/scroll/video behavior changes.

1. THREE-LAYER ARCHITECTURE:
   L0 BACKDROP — films where they exist, PLUS a new site-wide
   ambient backdrop behind all flat sections: fixed-position
   layer with a very subtle ink-tinted radial wash that drifts
   slowly (CSS keyframes, 90s loop, transform-only) over faint
   ledger baselines at 3%. Static under reduced-motion. This
   gives every glass surface real content to frost.
   L1 GLASS — all panels/cards/rows (below).
   L2 TEXT — unchanged tokens.
2. GLASS TOKENS (index.css utilities): .glass-paper (paper 68%,
   blur 16px saturate 1.15, 1px paper/45 border, 1px inner top
   highlight), .glass-ink (ink 55%, blur 14px, paper/15 border).
   Mobile <768px: blur 8px. @supports-not fallback: bg opacity
   92%. Sharp corners, ≤4px radius, no new shadows.
3. SURFACE CONVERSION — exhaustive:
   a. HERO LEDGER CARD (the recovery file board) → .glass-paper,
      the marquee surface; its deck edges become glass outlines.
   b. Header (scrolled), command palette, assist tab + sheet
      header, toasts → glass-paper (sheet body stays opaque for
      reading comfort).
   c. WHY ARG: the six rows wrap in ONE .glass-paper section
      panel (rows separated by rules inside it) — one blur plane,
      not six. SAME single-panel rule for: Process steps,
      Estimator instrument panel, FAQ accordion, careers
      listings, blog list rows, contact direct-line block, hours
      table, what-happens-next, locality card.
   d. Forms: inputs/selects/textarea become glass-paper fields
      with rule borders; focus = recovered ring, unchanged.
   e. Industries tiles panel over the skyline, trust-strip and
      closing-CTA text blocks → glass-ink.
   f. Footer: columns wrap in one glass-ink panel over the
      backdrop (the ink band itself may lighten to 85% so the
      backdrop breathes through).
4. PERFORMANCE LAW (critical at this scale): max FIVE
   backdrop-filter surfaces composited per viewport at any scroll
   position — the single-panel-per-section rule is how we meet
   it; audit every scroll position and demote violations to
   higher-opacity non-blur. Glass surfaces must not sit inside
   per-frame animated transforms (reveals animate the panel as a
   unit — fine; scrub-scaling a glass panel re-rasterizes — the
   hero card's pin-scrub scale: verify fps with ?debugfps, and if
   it drops below 55, freeze the card's scale during scrub and
   keep only its translate).
